# Mr. Mayor: Build the City! Rules

Version: 2026-10-18 (before the redesign requested by the teacher).

## Goal
Be the mayor of a small city. Build it to meet a starting mission, then adapt it to 3 event cards. If the city satisfies the mission and all 3 cards, you are Best Mayor.

## Components
- 6x6 board with roads. 36 cells. 11 road cells cross in the middle, leaving 25 buildable cells in 4 zones (A-D).
- Pieces (5 types only): House (residential), Building (general development), Shop (local business), Green Space (parks), Factory (industry).
- 15 event cards, starting mission cards, optional timer (default 120 s, adjustable).

## Adjacency
Adjacent means sharing a side (up, down, left, right). Diagonal does not count. A piece is adjacent to a road when it touches a road cell.

## Setup
Take a mission card and the 10 starting pieces: House 4, Building 2, Shop 2, Green 2, Factory 0. Place all of them on buildable cells. 15 cells stay empty.
Example missions (not final): every House touches a Green or Shop; every Shop touches a Road; every Green touches a House; every Building touches a Road.

## How to play
1. Get your mission.
2. Build your city. Think about space, roads and neighbours.
3. Draw 3 event cards, one at a time. Follow each card and adjust the city before drawing the next.
4. Check. The city must meet the mission and all 3 cards. All met = Best Mayor. Otherwise the mayor needs more work.

Actions: BUILD (add a piece), MOVE (place an existing piece elsewhere), REMOVE (take a piece off), RESTRICT (a placement limit).

## Drawing cards
- If a card needs something you do not have (card 07 needs a Factory), draw another.
- Never keep conflicting cards together: card 06 with 03, 04, 12, 13, 14.
- Cards must make the player adapt, never make the game impossible.
- Bonus lines are extra and not needed to win.

## Event cards
Types: Requirement (must build), Constraint (adds a limit), Opportunity (rebuild chance). Thai titles in brackets.

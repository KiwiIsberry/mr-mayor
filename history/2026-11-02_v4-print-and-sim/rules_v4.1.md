# Mr. Mayor — Rules v4.1 (2026-11-02)

You are the mayor for 12 rounds (3 rounds = 1 year, 4 years). Grow the city and protect it from disasters. After round 12, count stars. 8 stars = Best Mayor.

## Board
- 7×7 grid, columns A–G, rows 1–7. Roads on column D and row 4 → 36 lots in 4 zones (3×3 each).

| Zone (TH) | Zone (EN) | Lots | Home hazard |
|---|---|---|---|
| ย่านเก่า | Old Town | A–C, 1–3 | Fire |
| ริมแม่น้ำ | Riverside | E–G, 1–3 | Flood |
| ดินอ่อน | Soft Ground | A–C, 5–7 | Earthquake |
| ที่โล่ง | Open Plain | E–G, 5–7 | Storm |

## Setup
- 6 coins. House A1, House B2, Shop C1, Park A2.
- Shuffle event deck (33) and disaster deck (12). Keep the 6 factory-fire cards aside.

## Buildings
| Building | Lots | Cost (= repair) | Income / round | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| House บ้าน | 1 | 4 | 1 | housing 1 |
| Shop ร้านค้า | 1 | 7 | 2 | must share a side with a house/apartments |
| Factory โรงงาน | 2 | 8 | 4 | must share a side with a house/apartments · risk +1 · shuffle 1 factory-fire card into the disaster deck |
| Apartments คอนโด | 2 | 10 | 1 | housing 4 |
| Park สวน | 1 | 4 | – | never damaged |
- "Share a side" = left/right/up/down, not diagonal. Checked only when building.
- 2-lot buildings can rotate; cannot cross a road.
- Damaged building: no income, no stars until repaired (repair = full cost).
- Demolish: free, no refund (removing a factory: risk −1 and remove 1 factory-fire card).

## Each round
1. Open 2 event cards; apply both.
2. Collect income from every undamaged building.
3. Roll 2 dice (not in round 1). Total ≤ risk → draw a disaster card and do what it says.
4. Manage the city: build, repair, buy Totems, reinforce, demolish — as much as you can afford.
5. End the round.

Risk = 4 + 2 per past year + 1 per factory (max 12) → year 1–4: 4 / 6 / 8 / 10.

## Disaster cards (12)
A card names a hazard, a zone and 2 building types: every building of those types in that zone is damaged.

| Hazard | Damages | Cards |
|---|---|---|
| Flood | house, shop | Riverside ×2, Soft Ground ×1 |
| Earthquake | apartments, factory | Soft Ground ×2, Open Plain ×1 |
| Storm | house, factory | Open Plain ×2, Old Town ×1 |
| Fire | shop, apartments | Old Town ×2, Riverside ×1 |
| Factory fire | a random factory + every building touching it | +1 card per factory |
Draw and discard; reshuffle when empty.

## Protection
- **Totem** (one of each, permanent, blocks the whole card in every zone). Price rises with each one bought: **8 → 12 → 16 → 20**.
  - Flood wall คันกั้นน้ำ (flood) · Quake engineers วิศวกรโครงสร้าง (earthquake) · Storm warning siren ศูนย์เตือนพายุ (storm) · Firefighters / fire truck นักดับเพลิง (fire + factory fire)
- **Reinforce** เสริมความแข็งแรง: 3 coins, token on 1 building (max 1 each). The next time it would be damaged, it isn't; remove the token.

# Mr. Mayor: Build the City!

Physical 3D city-building board game for an SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities) exhibition booth.
Full rules and the 15 event cards are in `rules.md` (read it only when needed).

## Working agreements
- Owner: Alex (Chokun). Alex decides the rules. Friends suggest; Alex approves.
- Write shared docs in English first. Add Thai only where it helps (2 teammates are international and cannot read Thai).
- Keep this file short (under about 100 lines). Move old material to `archive/` instead of deleting.
- At the end of a working session, update Status, Decisions and Next steps below.
- Only 5 piece types exist: House, Building, Shop, Green Space, Factory. Do not add new pieces unless marked as an optional idea.

## Status (updated 2026-10-18)
- Current game: 6x6 board, cross-shaped roads (11 road cells, 25 buildable, 4 zones A-D), start with 10 pieces (House 4, Building 2, Shop 2, Green 2, Factory 0), draw 3 of 15 event cards, win if the starting mission and all 3 cards are satisfied.
- Playable prototype (HTML, pre-redesign version): https://claude.ai/artifact/S2MQZo9i2cLkzRrKYiCS5p
- Teacher feedback (Oct 7): interesting, but too short (about 3 minutes of play) and too wide/shallow. She wants ONE specific SDG 11 problem and more depth. Up to 20 minutes is allowed (a maximum, not a target).
- The game needs major changes. The single focus problem is not chosen yet.

## Open questions
- Which single SDG 11 problem to focus on? Options discussed: (A) green space, heat and flooding (recommended, needs no new pieces), (B) housing for everyone, (C) tourism pressure vs local residents.
- Solo or team play? Win/lose or a score?
- Building: 1 cell or 1x2?
- Cards 06, 08, 09, 11 pass with no action. Duplicate logic: 02=05, 04=14, 03=13. Bonuses (13, 15) have no effect without scoring.
- Ideas proposed, not decided: rules that stack across rounds, role cards with conflicting goals, a 3-measure result (cool homes, flood-safe zones, livelihood) instead of pass/fail. None of this is playtested.

## Booth facts
- Event: November 17, multi-group exhibition in one hall. One table, 3 m wide. 5+ team members.
- Budget: roughly a few hundred to low thousand baht, not final.
- Mood: fun and lighthearted. Visitors play on their own, staff supervise.
- Prizes: Best Mayor gets a crown or ribbon, a photo with their city and a souvenir. Everyone who plays gets a souvenir.
